Rune Grammar

Every drawing of one to three runes, and what it casts today. Most of them have no name yet — they fall through to the blend rule and fire their parts at once. Those are the slots worth arguing about.

Generated by tools/rune_sheet.py from spellbook.gd and miracle_manager.gd, so nothing here is aspirational — it is what the build actually does, costed and weighed with the game’s own formulas. The stroke shapes are drawn from the recognizer’s own templates, so they cannot drift from what the game will accept.

New this pass: four workings, two of which are the first that change the shape of the world rather than what is standing on it — earth + fury is an earthquake, earth + fire + fury a volcano. Alongside them, ward + water gives the creature footing on water and calm + life + water is a healing shower. All four are priced low on purpose while they are being played with.

What needs deciding

Three questions for testers

  • The 236 blends. Any drawing with no name casts each rune’s own miracle at once, weakened. Which of these deserve to be their own thing? fire + water ought to mean something — steam, surely. So ought calm + water, now that its three-rune sibling calm + life + water is a healing shower.
  • Seven drawings are pure waste — a shorter one already casts exactly the same thing, at the same potency, for the same or less prayer. Filter to Redundant to see them. They should either do something of their own or stop existing. (Note this is a stricter test than it looks: fire + fire is not here, because a second fire rune buys a bigger fireball rather than the same one.)
  • Fury is earning its place now. It appears in seven named recipes and genuinely changes the outcome in four of them — it was one of five before the earth-movers arrived. The three that still do nothing are air + air + fury, air + fire + fury and air + air + fury + water, where the fury is simply ignored. So the old question is half-answered: fury reads as an ingredient after all. Should the three dead ones be given something to do, or dropped?
